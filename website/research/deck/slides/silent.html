03 · The cost of a wrong answer

A wrong quantum answer looks exactly like a right one

Noise

At 99.9% gate fidelity, a 10,000-gate circuit expects ~10 faults

Drift

The device shifts between calibration and your run

Software bugs

The circuit that ran isn't the one you specified

Corner-cutting

Fewer shots, shallower circuits, smaller models

Classical stand-ins

A cheap approximation returned as a quantum result

Fraud

In an open market, a plausible fake costs nothing

None of these raises an error. Every one returns a plausible number.
