03 · The cost of a wrong answer

Unchecked computations have been expensive before

Intel

1994

$475M charge

A flaw in the Pentium's division unit returned wrong results in rare cases.

JPMorgan Chase

2012

~$6.2B in losses

London Whale trades: spreadsheet errors in the risk model understated risk.

Google · Meta

2021

No error raised

CPUs in their fleets silently returned wrong results during normal work.

All three were classical, so checking caught them eventually. A wrong quantum result offers nothing to check against.

Sources: Intel, Jan 1995 · JPMorgan Chase Management Task Force report, 2013 · Hochschild et al. (Google), HotOS 2021 · Dixit et al. (Meta), 2021
