03 · The cost of a wrong answer

A wrong answer costs more than the run

False positive

The wrong molecule advances

Synthesis, assays and animal studies are spent on a compound that was never going to work.

False negative

The right molecule is dropped

The candidate that would have worked is discarded, and nobody ever finds out.

Hidden costs

Errors compound

Wrong energies train ML models, calibrate force fields and seed the next screen.

No audit trail

The team can't show partners or investors why the decision was sound.

Adoption stalls

Unverifiable results get treated as hints, not decisions, so quantum's value is never captured.
