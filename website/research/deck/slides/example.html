02 · Quantum in the cloud

One number comes out, with a quantum state behind it

The question

How strongly does candidate A bind the enzyme's iron-containing active site?

Why quantum

Electrons around the iron are strongly correlated. Classical approximations disagree with each other, so the team sends this one molecule to a quantum cloud.

What comes back

ΔE = −12.4

kcal/mol. One classical number (illustrative).

What stands behind it

2¹⁰⁰ ≈ 10³⁰

complex amplitudes in the ground state of a ~100-qubit model. Only a quantum computer can hold it.
